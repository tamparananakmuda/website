## Problem Framing Framework (5 lapis)

Whitepaper TAM bukan sekadar "topik menarik". Setiap whitepaper harus mulai dari problem framing yang tajam:

### Lapis 1: Surface Problem (gejala)
Apa yang terlihat di permukaan? Apa yang orang keluhkan?
- Contoh: "Gen Z sering resign dalam 6 bulan pertama"

### Lapis 2: Structural Problem (sistem)
Sistem atau struktur apa yang menyebabkan gejala ini?
- Contoh: "Sistem rekrutmen tidak match ekspektasi dengan realitas kerja"

### Lapis 3: Root Cause (akar masalah)
Apa akar masalah struktural ini? Kenapa sistemnya begini?
- Contoh: "Pendidikan tidak menyiapkan skill kerja, industri tidak investasi dalam onboarding"

### Lapis 4: Stakeholder Mapping (siapa untung/rugi)
Siapa yang diuntungkan dari status quo? Siapa yang dirugikan?
- Contoh: "Perusahaan untung dari turnover (gaji murah, fresh grad), Gen Z rugi (tidak stabil, tidak berkembang)"

### Lapis 5: TAM Angle (kontra-narasi)
Apa narasi publik yang salah tentang masalah ini? Apa yang TAM akan katakan yang berbeda?
- Contoh: "Narasi publik: Gen Z tidak loyal. TAM: sistem kerja yang eksploitatif, bukan Gen Z yang bermasalah"

Isi semua 5 lapis. Jika tidak bisa sampai Lapis 5, topik belum cukup matang untuk whitepaper.

## Hypothesis Generation (3-5 hypothesis)

Sebelum riset, generate 3-5 hypothesis yang akan diuji di step 02-research:

| Hypothesis | Tipe | Cara uji |
|------------|------|----------|
| H1: [claim utama] | Kontra-narasi | Data BPS/OJK + literatur akademik |
| H2: [claim pendukung] | Kausal | Korelasi data + studi existing |
| H3: [claim konteks] | Deskriptif | Data demografi + survei |
| H4: [claim prediktif] | Proyeksi | Trend data + model sederhana |
| H5: [null hypothesis] | Negatif | Cari data yang BISA membantah thesis |

H5 (null hypothesis) WAJIB. Whitepaper yang tidak punya null hypothesis = konfirmasi bias, bukan riset.

## Deep Analysis Frameworks (20x Depth)

Setiap whitepaper TAM harus melalui framework analisis berikut. Pilih minimal 5 framework yang paling relevan dengan topik. Epistemology Check wajib semua.

### Epistemology Check (wajib semua)

**Popper Falsifiability Test:** Setiap hypothesis harus punya potential falsifier. Tanya: "Apa data yang bisa membuktikan claim ini salah?" Jika tidak ada, claim bukan ilmiah. Hindari ad hoc rationalization (immunize claim dari falsification).

**Kuhn Anomaly Identification:** Identifikasi anomalies (data/fenomena yang tidak cocok narasi populer). Akumulasi anomalies = basis "tamparan". Show anomalies dulu, bukan langsung argue for new paradigm.

**Lakatos Progressive Check:** Thesis harus predicts novel facts (sesuatu yang belum orang pikirkan). Jika hanya explain retroactively = degenerating programme (lemah). Hard core (central thesis) + protective belt (auxiliary hypotheses).

**Toulmin Argument Planning:** Setiap major argument: Claim (thesis) + Ground (evidence) + Warrant (logic bridge, sering missing di whitepaper lemah) + Backing (credentials) + Qualifier (degree of certainty) + Rebuttal (conditions where claim tidak hold).

**Aristotle Rhetoric Balance:** Ethos (credibility, TAM dominant) + Pathos (emotion, terbatas, tidak manipulasi) + Logos (data/logika, utama). Whitepaper = deliberative rhetoric (rekomendasi masa depan). Enthymeme = argument dengan implicit premise yang reader lengkapi.

### Thinking Frameworks (pilih minimal 3)

**McKinsey Problem Structuring:** MECE issue tree (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive). 4 breakdown methods: math-based, segment, process, stakeholder. Hypothesis-driven: tulis "Day 1 answer" sebelum riset. ROOTS check: Root cause? Owned? Objective? Testable? Time-bound? Specific?

**Pyramid Principle Pre-Structure:** SCQA pembuka: Situation (konteks), Complication (masalah), Question (pertanyaan), Answer (thesis awal). Governing thought = front-loaded thesis. 3-5 supporting arguments MECE. "Think bottom-up, present top-down."

**First Principles Decomposition:** Deconstruct topic ke fundamental truths. Reason up dari sana. Identify "Idiot Index": common wisdom yang TAM challenge. Cartesian Doubt: doubt everything until indubitable.

**TRIZ Contradiction Identification:** Identify trade-offs dalam topic (e.g., stabilitas vs kebebasan). 40 inventive principles untuk generate novel solutions. Ideal Final Result (IFR): apa versi ideal tanpa compromise?

**Lateral Thinking Ideation:** Six Thinking Hats: White (fakta), Red (feeling), Black (risiko), Yellow (benefit), Green (kreatif), Blue (proses). Random entry, provocation techniques untuk generate "tamparan" angle.

**Cynefin Domain Classification:** Clear (best practice), Complicated (good practice, expert analysis), Complex (emergent practice, safe-to-fail experiments), Chaotic (novel practice, immediate action), Confusion (break down situation first). Match approach ke domain.

**Abductive Reasoning:** Observasi surprising fact > generate hypothesis (thesis) > derive testable consequences > test di 02-research > verify di 03-strategy. Abduction = generate "tamparan" insight yang counter-intuitive.

**Design Thinking Adoption:** Empathize (pahami target reader) > Define (pain points) > Ideate (multiple angles) > Prototype (draft) > Test (review). User-centered, bukan author-centered.

### Persuasion Psychology Mapping

**ELM Route Selection:** Elaboration Likelihood Model. Central route (evaluasi argumen, attitude change durable) vs Peripheral route (heuristic cues, attitude change temporary). TAM "tamparan" = central route persuasion: logical argument yang membuat reader berpikir ulang. Bukan emotional manipulation (peripheral).

**5 Persuasion Principles:** Reciprocity (+47-87% compliance), Authority (+43% persuasion rate), Scarcity (+30-39%), Social Proof, Commitment/Consistency. TAM pakai Authority (data, expert quotes) + Commitment (reader yang setuju dengan 1 claim akan cenderung setuju dengan claim berikutnya).

**Cognitive Dissonance Strategy:** TAM "tamparan" = cognitive dissonance yang sehat. Present data yang bertentangan dengan keyakinan reader. Dissonance = motivasi untuk resolve (ubah keyakinan atau reject data). TAM design untuk reader pilih: ubah keyakinan berdasar data.

**Self-Validation Mechanism:** Reader yang validasi pikiran mereka sendiri tentang argument = lebih persuasif. TAM: biarkan reader menarik conclusion sendiri dari data. Jangan spell out "jadi kamu harus berpikir seperti ini."

Pilih narrative architecture sebelum riset dimulai:

### Behavioral & Cognitive Design

**Prospect Theory Framing:** Loss framing untuk "tamparan": "apa yang kamu sudah punya dan bisa hilang". Loss aversion: loss ~2x lebih impactful dari gain. Accurate not manipulative. Reference point: outcomes relative to current state.

**Nudge Theory Positioning:** Libertarian paternalism: present options + consequences, biarkan reader decide. TAM tidak menggurui = nudge. Choice architecture: design presentation yang mengarah ke better decision.

**Cognitive Load Planning:** Working memory: 5-9 elements per section. 3 load types: intrinsic (complexity), extraneous (format noise), germane (schema building). Worked example effect: konkret > abstract. Split-attention avoidance.

**Information Foraging Design:** Information scent: strong titles, bolded findings, pull quotes. Patch model: reader stay vs leave berdasarkan value-per-effort. Reward frequency: key insight setiap 200-300 kata.

**Dual Process Targeting:** System 1 (fast): cognitive ease, visual hierarchy, social proof. System 2 (slow): logical argument, data. Anchoring: first claim = anchor. Framing: cara data presented affects interpretation.

**Shannon Information Density:** Whitepaper = communication channel. Signal = key insights. Noise = filler, jargon, repetition. Maximize signal-to-noise ratio. Hapus paragraph yang tidak menambah information.

### Narrative & Semiotic Planning

**Campbell Hero's Journey Arc (opsional):** Reader = hero. Problem = call to adventure. Data = road of trials. Insight = ultimate boon. Recommendation = return with elixir. Adaptasi struktural, bukan literal.

**Narrative Transportation Design:** Case studies dengan narrative arc = transportation (absorption). Transportation reduces counter-arguing. TAM tetap fact-based: transportation via structure, bukan fabrication.

**Barthes Myth Deconstruction:** Identifikasi "natural truth" yang sebenarnya cultural construct. Denotation (literal) vs connotation (cultural) vs myth (ideology disguised as natural). TAM "tamparan" = demystification.

**McLuhan Medium Analysis:** "The medium is the message". Digital whitepaper (scrollable, searchable, linkable) vs PDF (linear) = different cognitive effects. Design untuk medium digital: scannable, searchable, linkable.

### Sociology & Systems Analysis

**Social Construction Mapping:** "Reality" generasi muda = socially constructed. Externalization > objectivation > internalization. TAM: show construction process, biarkan reader re-evaluate. Tidak relativist: beberapa realities lebih accurate.

**Bourdieu Capital Dynamics:** 3 forms capital: economic, cultural (knowledge/skills), social (connections). "Success" = function of capital distribution, bukan pure merit. TAM: reveal invisible capital dynamics.

**Foucault Discourse Identification:** Discourse: practices that form objects of which they speak. Power/knowledge: power produces knowledge. TAM: identify discourse yang shape reader's thinking. Who benefits from current discourse?

**Meadows Leverage Point Analysis:** 12 leverage points (least to most effective): parameters > buffers > stock/flow > delays > balancing loops > reinforcing loops > info flows > rules > self-organize > goals > paradigm > transcend paradigms. Target high-leverage points. "Tamparan" = reveal counterintuitive leverage point.

**Systems Thinking Mapping:** Feedback loops (balancing vs reinforcing). Stocks and flows. Emergence: behavior from interactions. System traps: limits to growth, shifting burden, tragedy of commons, fixes that fail.

### Research Methodology Pre-Selection

**Qualitative Method:** Grounded Theory (topik baru, generate theory dari data), Ethnography (kultur audience), Case Study (kasus spesifik), Phenomenology (lived experience), Narrative (cerita individu).

**Mixed Methods:** Convergent parallel (qual+quant bersamaan), Exploratory sequential (qual dulu, quant validate), Explanatory sequential (quant dulu, qual explain). Integration points explicit.

**Action Research Spiral:** Plan (riset) > Act (publish) > Observe (reader response) > Reflect > re-plan. Iterative improvement. Whitepaper = living document.

**Data Journalism Standards (CAR):** Computer-Assisted Reporting. Data bulletproofing: consistency-check, cross-source verification, work log. Open methodology: "nerd box". Reproducibility.

**Meta-Analytic Thinking:** Effect sizes > p-values: "berapa besar effect" bukan hanya "apakah significant". Heterogeneity: acknowledge variation. Publication bias check. Forest-plot thinking: visualize range across sources.

**Writing Process Planning:** Writing = iterative problem-solving (Flower & Hayes), bukan linear draft. Plan > translate > review > re-plan. Goals evolve during writing. Monitor = metacognitive check: "apakah ini masih aligned dengan TAM tone? Apakah claim masih proportionate to evidence?" Expert writers = more recursive, more goal-modifying. Novice writers = too linear, too committed to initial plan. Rencanakan untuk iterative, bukan one-shot.

### Content Strategy & Distribution

**Whitepaper Type Selection:**

| Tipe | Kapan |
|------|-------|
| Problem/Solution | Identifikasi masalah + propose solution |
| Backgrounder | Educate on complex topic |
| Numbered List | 5-10 key findings/predictions |
| Hybrid | Mix dari above |

**Audience Persona Segmentation:** Primary reader (80%, decision-maker 25-40) + Secondary reader (20%, influencer/Gen Z). Psychographic > firmographic. Decision journey: awareness, consideration, decision.

**Content Funnel Positioning:** TAM = TOFU/MOFU hybrid (educate + provoke). Derivative assets mapped per funnel stage. CTA per stage.

**Content Atomization Plan:** 15+ derivatives: 3 blog, 5 quote cards, 2 carousels, 1 infographic, 1 thread, 1 newsletter, 1 video, 1 podcast. Pre-production atomization.

**E-E-A-T Signal Planning:** Named author, verifiable credentials, first-person experience, primary sources, expert quotes, visible dates, transparent methodology.

**Gating Strategy:** TAM = ungated (maximize AI visibility, SEO, social). CTA = engagement, bukan email capture.

**AI Search Optimization:** Front-loaded thesis (1 paragraph page 1). Semantic heading structure. Citable passage per section. Statistical formatting (angka spesifik).

**Pipeline Automation Planning:** Claim extraction + validation gate. Version control via git. Audit trail. Fact-check automation. AI SEO check per section.

### Risk & Quality Guardrails

**Failure Mode Risk Register:**

| Risk | Mitigasi |
|------|----------|
| Terlalu sales-pitch | 95/5 rule: 95% education, 5% promotion |
| Messaging tidak stabil | Stability check per section |
| Scope creep | Scope statement di awal |
| Data lemah | Min 2 source per claim, triangulation |
| Tone tidak konsisten | TAM voice check per section |
| Terlalu boros | Information density audit |

**Think Tank Rigor:** Problem framing transparency. Acknowledge POV. Independent review plan. Formal pipeline: framing > acquisition > analysis > review > publication.

**Policy Standard Alignment:** 3 mandatory questions (What is the problem? What is the evidence? What is the recommendation?). 2-sentence argument test. Plain language mandate.
