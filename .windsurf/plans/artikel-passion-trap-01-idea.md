## Substansi (Berbobot, Sederhana)

Topik ini punya backing riset kuat yang bisa dijelaskan sederhana:

1. **Cal Newport ("So Good They Can't Ignore You")** — passion datang setelah expertise, bukan sebelum. Follow passion pertama = karier nggak stabil.
2. **O'Keefe, Dweck & Walton (Stanford/Yale, 2018)** — "find your passion" creates fixed mindset, makes people give up when things get hard.
3. **Vallerand (2002)** — only 4% of passions relate to work. 96% are hobbies.
4. **Class privilege** — "follow your passion" assumes safety net. Gen Z tanpa modal keluarga nggak mampu "coba-coba".
5. **Employer benefit** — perusahaan pakai "passion" untuk justifikasi unpaid internships, gaji rendah, overtime.
6. **Indonesia context** — 35% pekerja muda mismatch (BPS 2024), 8 juta sarjana overeducated (LPEM FEB UI 2026).

Bahasa penyajiannya akan sederhana: "Passion nggak bayar kos. Passion nggak bayar BPJS. Passion nggak bayar tagihan listrik."
