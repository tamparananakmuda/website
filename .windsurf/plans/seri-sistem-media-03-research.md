## DATA PENDUKUNG PER PART

### Part 1: Konsentrasi Kepemilikan
1. ~10 large media groups control mainstream market: MNC (Hary Tanoesoedibjo/Perindo), Media Group (Surya Paloh/Nasdem), Visi Media Asia (Aburizal Bakrie/Golkar), Mahaka Media (Erick Thohir), Trans Corp (Chairul Tanjung), KG Media (Kompas), SCMA/EMTEK (Edy Sariaatmadja), B Universe (James Riady/Lippo) — RSF 2025 + ISEAS 2024/29 (T1)
2. 8 politically-connected individuals control media industry, all with direct political party affiliations — ISEAS 2024/29 (T1)
3. Indonesia press freedom rank: 127/180 (down 16 spots), score 44.13 "difficult situation" — RSF 2025 (T1)
4. 5,019 media companies in 2024: 3,886 online (77.43%), 549 radio, 527 newspapers, 47 local TV, 10 national TV — Press Council via RSF 2025 (T1)
5. Only ~1,800 media verified by Press Council out of ~50,000 — Press Council 2025 (T1)
6. "Media oligarchy linked to political interests has grown stronger" — RSF 2025 (T1)

### Part 2: Ekonomi Klik
1. 1,200 media workers laid off 2023-2024 — Press Council via Jakarta Globe 2024 (T2)
2. Total ad spend 2024: Rp 71.5T. 80% to Google/Meta, 20% to publishers — AMSI/IDC 2024 (T1)
3. Ad spend 2025 projected Rp 75T, only ~20% to publishers — Wavemaker via Bisnis.com 2024 (T2)
4. Clickbait dataset: all 12 online media use clickbait, 8 mainstream. Tribunnews most criticized — ISEAS 2024/29 (T1)
5. 35.9% Indonesian internet users install ad-blockers — Nielsen Norman Group via Jakarta Post 2025 (T2)
6. Only 19% Indonesians willing to pay for online news — ISEAS 2024/29 (T1)
7. Kompas Gramedia uses Tribunnews clickbait to offset "good" journalism costs — ISEAS 2024/29 (T1)
8. 549 journalists PHK in 2025 (up from 373 in 2024) — AJI 2025 (T1)

### Part 3: Algoritma sebagai Editor
1. TikTok: 112M active users in Indonesia, predominantly 18-24 — Konvergensi Journal 2025 (T1)
2. TikTok algorithm creates echo chambers: emotional/dramatization content > factual for virality — Konvergensi Journal 2025 (T1)
3. TikTok admitted "heating" practice (0.002% claimed) — ISEAS 2025/52 (T1)
4. Survey: 62% recognize TikTok content as populist, 63% encounter Prabowo-Gibran most — Springer 2025 (T1)
5. "Objektif algoritma: pengguna menghabiskan waktu selama mungkin di platform" — Enda Nasution via VOA 2024 (T2)
6. 106M TikTok users Oct 2023, 2nd largest after US — We Are Social via VOA 2024 (T2)
7. Algorithm personalizes For You page based on engagement, not source credibility — Konvergensi Journal 2025 (T1)

### Part 4: Ekosistem Hoax
1. Buzzer networks charge jutaan to miliaran per project; 1 coordinator claims 70,000 buzzers — Kompas investigation 2025 (T1)
2. SMM Panel: Rp 100,000 = 2,000 followers + 500 comments — Kompas 2025 (T1)
3. Phone farming: 1 box (20 phones) = 200 accounts, scalable to 1,800. Detected as "real device" — Kompas 2025 (T1)
4. Buzzer Robert: $10,000/month, industry "slicker and professional" — ABC News 2024 (T2)
5. Kominfo: 200+ hoaxes across 3,000 posts on FB, X, IG, TikTok — Kominfo 2024 (T1)
6. AI generative makes buzzer content hard to distinguish — Drone Emprit via Kompas 2025 (T2)
7. Buzzers recruited via civic organizations, university students paid cheaply — ISEAS 2024/66 (T1)
8. Mafindo: buzzer content uses "gray area" rumors — Septiaji via Kompas 2025 (T2)

### Part 5: Influencer sebagai Jurnalis
1. "Pers ada kode etik, influencer/YouTuber tidak ada yang mengawasi" — Ninik Rahayu (Dewan Pers) 2024 (T1)
2. "Pemerintah seharusnya tidak membayar influencer, menempatkan iklan di media massa" — Bayu Wardhana (AJI) 2025 (T1)
3. "Influencer punya kedekatan emosional yang tidak dimiliki media arus utama" — Rosarita Niken (Dewan Pers) 2025 (T1)
4. Public trust in media: 36% (2025, up from 35%). Kompas highest at 62% — Reuters Institute 2025 (T1)
5. "Batas jurnalis dan content creator semakin kabur" — Wagub Jateng 2025 (T2)
6. "Influencer hanya perpanjangan tangan humas kementerian" — AJI 2025 (T1)
7. BBC Media Action: 5,036 respondents, political parties perceived as negatively impacting media credibility — BBC 2025 (T1)

### Part 6: Kematian Jurnalisme Lokal
1. 1,200 journalists PHK 2023-2024. 549 in 2025 alone — Press Council + AJI 2025 (T1)
2. 75% national ad revenue taken by global digital platforms — Dewan Pers 2024 (T1)
3. Media biro daerah tutup, 3 tahap PHK akhir 2024-2025 — AJI "Pecat dan Bungkam" 2025 (T1)
4. 4 types media death: by closure, by silence, by hollowing, zombie media — Keadilan.id 2025 (T2)
5. "News deserts" concept — Abernathy 2018 via Keadilan.id (T3)
6. 89 cases journalist violence 2025, 29 digital attacks (highest 12 years) — AJI 2025 (T1)
7. Media lokal depend on APBD/APBN, efisiensi pemerintah cuts revenue — AJI 2025 (T1)
8. Jurnalis dibayar di bawah UMR, kontrak berkepanjangan, sistem kemitraan — AJI via Tempo 2025 (T1)

### Part 7: Sintesis
1. Trust in information disorder increased post-2024 election — CSIS/SAIL 2024 (T1)
2. Political literacy negatively associated with trust in disinfo, but trust still increased — CSIS/SAIL 2024 (T1)
3. Public trust in media: 36% (2025), below 2021 levels — Reuters Institute 2025 (T1)
4. RSF 2025: economic indicator lowest in history. 160/180 countries "with difficulty" or "not at all" — RSF 2025 (T1)
5. Indonesia press freedom: 127/180, economic fragility ranking 142nd — RSF 2025 (T1)
6. Trust in disinfo → dissatisfied with democracy, don't support democratic system — CSIS/SAIL 2024 (T1)
7. "Media owners 'always' or 'often' limited editorial independence" in over half countries — RSF 2025 (T1)
8. Media ownership highly concentrated in 46 countries, Indonesia named — RSF 2025 (T1)
